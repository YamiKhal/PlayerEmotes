How to actually play emotes, everything from the wheel to commands.

## The Emote Wheel

**Press B** to open it. Click a slot to play that emote, or press `1` to `8`.


    Slots - every slot shows a 3D preview with your own skin, it starts moving when you hover it
    Pages - 10 pages of slots, flip through them with the arrows (or scroll)
    Stop Emote - stops what you're playing
    All Emotes - every emote you have, sorted in packs
    Edit Wheel - put emotes in whatever slot you want
    Packs... - hide or show whole emote packs
    Settings... - everything in Settings

If you like holding the key and letting go over an emote more, turn on *Hold to Open Wheel* in the settings.
A quick tap still keeps it open.

## Putting emotes on the wheel

1. Click **Edit Wheel**.
2. Pick an emote on the left.
3. Click the slot you want it in. **Right-click** a slot to clear it.

## All Emotes

Every emote you have, one section per pack (A-Z). Hover a pack header to see who made it.

- Click an emote to preview it, click it again to play it.
- *Recently Used* at the top keeps your last 10 emotes. It's folded at first, click it to open.
- The search box searches names.

## Stopping an emote

You don't need to do anything special. **Walking, jumping or attacking stops it**, so an emote never gets in your
way. *Stop Emote* in the wheel works too, and so does pressing the same hotkey again.

## Keys

All of them are in *Options > Controls > Key Binds > PlayerEmotes*.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|-----|---------|--------------|
| Open Emote Wheel | `B` | opens the wheel |
| Play Wheel Slot 1-4 | unassigned | plays slot 1-4 of the first page, press again to stop |
| Accept Partner Emote | unassigned | joins a partner emote someone asked you to |
| Reload Emote Files | unassigned | reloads emote files, for making emotes |

## Commands

```
/emote <emote>               play an emote
/emotesync <player>          join the emote a player near you is playing
/emoteaccept                 accept a partner emote request
```

`/emote` tab completes. You can write the full id (`playeremotes:wave`) or just `wave`.
All the ids are in the [Emote List](Emote-List).

## Camera

If you start an emote in first person, the camera goes to third person so you can actually see yourself, and back
once you're done. Don't like it? Turn off *Third Person While Emoting* in the [Settings](Settings).

## Controllers

With [Controlify](https://modrinth.com/mod/controlify) the cursor snaps to the wheel's slots and buttons. You can
bind the mod's keys to controller buttons in Controlify's settings.

Without a mouse, the wheel also works with `Tab`, the arrow keys and `Enter`.
