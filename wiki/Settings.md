Open the wheel (**B**) and click **Settings...** in the bottom left.

Every option also has a tooltip in game, hover it.

## Sound

    My Emote Sounds - whether your emotes play their sound, for you and for players near you
    Other Players' Emote Sounds - whether you hear the sounds of other players' emotes

## Other Players

    Other Players' Emotes - whether you see other players' emotes at all. When off, nobody can play emotes on your screen
    Partner Emote Requests - whether others can ask you to join their partner emotes. You can still join one yourself

## Looks

    Emote Previews - 3D previews of each emote in the wheel and lists, or only names
    Third Person While Emoting - goes to third person when you start an emote, and back when it ends
    Elbows and Knees - how emotes that bend the arms and legs look

**Elbows and Knees** has 4 options:

| Option | What it looks like |
|--------|--------------------|
| As Made | the way the emote was made (default) |
| Smooth | one limb that bends in the middle |
| Split | two halves turning into each other, like in Blockbench |
| Off | straight arms and legs, the classic look |

Most of my emotes don't bend at all, so this mostly matters for emote packs. If another mod makes your limbs look
weird while emoting, set it to **Off**.

## Controls

    Hold to Open Wheel - the wheel stays open while you hold the key, let go over an emote to play it

## Packs

**Packs...** next to Settings lets you hide or show whole emote packs. Hidden packs don't show in the wheel or the
lists.
