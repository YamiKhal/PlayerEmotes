Some emotes need two people, like the hug, the high fives or the fusion dance. And when you see someone dancing,
you can join them in step.

_Both of these need the mod on the server. If you want to make your own partner emotes, see
[Making Partner Emotes](Making-Partner-Emotes)._

## Doing one with a friend

1. Stand in front of your friend, at most 2 blocks away, and **look at each other**.
2. Play a partner emote. You start the first half of it and wait there.
3. Your friend gets a request in chat. They click **[Accept]**, use `/emoteaccept`, press the *Accept Partner
   Emote* key or the button under the wheel.
4. You both play your part, lined up properly.


## Nobody in front of you?

Just wait there. Anyone can walk up, face you and accept.

When a few players are waiting, the nearest one in front of you is the one you join.

The request waits 60 seconds by default, servers can change that.

## What ends it

Moving, attacking, getting hurt, dying or leaving ends it **for both of you**.

## Don't want requests?

Turn off *Partner Emote Requests* in the [Settings](Settings). Nobody can ask you then, but you can still walk up
to someone waiting and join them yourself.

## Syncing with someone's emote

See someone dancing? Type:

```
/emotesync <player name>
```

You join them **in step with them**, not from the start. They have to be near you (in your render distance).

Works with any emote, not only dances. You can't sync with a partner emote though, those are for only two.
